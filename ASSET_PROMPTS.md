## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** WebP (lossy, quality 85–90)
- **Dimensions:** 
  - Portrait/sidebar images: 800 × 1066px (3:4 ratio)
  - Full-screen backgrounds: 1920 × 1080px or 1200 × 1600px portrait
- **Colour profile:** sRGB
- **No text, logos, or watermarks** visible in any image
- All images should feel like premium editorial/campaign photography, not stock imagery
